Assistant Professor (*Profesor Asistente*) in the Computer Science Section at the Facultad de Matemática, Astronomía, Física y Computación (FaMAF), Universidad Nacional de Córdoba (March 2021 – Present).

Courses taught and responsibilities:
* **Databases (*Bases de Datos*)**: Data modeling, Relational Algebra, SQL, Transactions, Concurrency control, and DBMS architecture.
* **Discrete Mathematics II (*Matemática Discreta II*)**: Graph theory, advanced algorithms, trees, network flows, and combinatorial optimization.
* **Introduction to Logic (*Introducción a la Lógica*)**: Propositional and first-order logic, Lattice Theory, Formal Grammars, and Automata Theory.
* Designing practical problem sets, guiding laboratory assignments, grading coursework, and mentoring undergraduate research students.
