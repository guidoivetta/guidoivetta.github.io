Assistant Professor (Profesor Asistente / Ayudante) in the Computer Science Section at the Facultad de Matemática, Astronomía, Física y Computación (FaMAF), Universidad Nacional de Córdoba.

Responsibilities include:
* Designing practical exercises, laboratory sessions, and grading assignments.
* Guiding undergraduate students in hands-on programming, algorithms, machine learning, and natural language processing concepts.
* Mentoring students in undergraduate research projects and final degree theses (Trabajos Finales de Licenciatura).
