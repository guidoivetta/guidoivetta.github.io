Co-instructor and tutorial organizer delivering hands-on instruction for international researchers, graduate students, and practitioners:

* **ACL 2025 Tutorial**: *Navigating Ethical Challenges in NLP: Hands-on strategies for students and researchers* (Vienna, Austria). Covered methodologies to evaluate social biases, design responsible AI protocols, and manage ethical considerations in NLP research.
* **Khipu 2025 Practical Session**: *Socio-Cultural Evaluation of AI* (Latin American Meeting in AI). Hands-on Jupyter notebook training on measuring cultural alignment, regional stereotypes, and calibration in large language models.
