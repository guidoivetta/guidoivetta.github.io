Current benchmarks for logical reasoning in LLMs typically assume deduction operates independently from demographic priors. This work examines empirical evidence showing that social stereotypes influence intermediate reasoning steps and final predictions, and discusses interventions on reasoning traces to mitigate biased inferences.
