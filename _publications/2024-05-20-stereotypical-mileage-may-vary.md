Evaluating social biases and stereotypes in Natural Language Processing has gained significant momentum, but most resources originate from a single culture (primarily US-centric). This collaborative work analyzes practical hurdles when adapting and evaluating biases across multiple languages, demonstrating how cultural context fundamentally reshapes stereotypical associations and evaluation methodology.
