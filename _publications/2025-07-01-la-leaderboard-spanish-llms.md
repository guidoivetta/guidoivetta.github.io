To drive equitable AI representation for Spanish-speaking communities worldwide, La Leaderboard evaluates LLMs across multiple linguistic tasks, dialectal varieties, and regional contexts. The project provides transparent benchmarks and community datasets to foster linguistically inclusive language technologies.
