As NLP research quickly translates into deployed technologies, addressing ethical issues is essential. This tutorial provides concrete tools, real case studies, and hands-on guidance for navigating ethics in data collection, modeling, bias mitigation, and publication ethics, drawing from members of the ACL Ethics Committee.
