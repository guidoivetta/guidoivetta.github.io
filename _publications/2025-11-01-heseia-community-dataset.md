Most resources for evaluating social bias in LLMs lack community co-design. HESEIA is a community-sourced dataset developed through participatory workshops in secondary schools in Argentina. We detail the participatory methodology, analyze the identified bias dimensions, and demonstrate how current models reproduce these harms.
