## Projects

Ethics in AI Team @ Fundación Vía Libre

Fundación Vía Libre is a non-profit civil society organization based in Argentina dedicated to digital rights, technological autonomy, and algorithmic accountability. As Technical Lead of the Artificial Intelligence Team, I direct research on identifying, measuring, and mitigating societal biases and demographic harms in foundation models across Latin America.

EDIA & EDIA 2.0 (Multimodal)

An open-source auditing platform developed to empower civil society organizations, educators, and domain experts to evaluate gender, racial, and regional biases in text embeddings and generative models without needing deep machine learning engineering expertise. Featured as an interactive tutorial at ACM FAccT 2024 in Rio de Janeiro and presented at RightsCon 2023 in Costa Rica.

## Datasets & Benchmarks

LACES: Latin-American Community-Sourced Evaluation of Stereotypes

ACL Findings 2026

An adaptive, iterative data collection methodology that addresses the critical geo-cultural gap in NLP evaluation by crowdsourcing and validating regional stereotypes and demographic biases across Latin American varieties of Spanish.

ACL Anthology arXiv:2510.24958

HESEIA: Co-Designed Social Bias Benchmark in Secondary Schools

EMNLP 2025 (Oral)

A benchmark of 46,499 sentences co-designed directly within secondary school environments across Latin America, engaging 370 teachers and 5,000+ students across 189 schools to audit social biases across curriculum areas. Presented at EMNLP 2025 and featured at the India AI Impact Summit 2026.

EMNLP Paper Project Report

INCLUDE-2.0: Region-Aware Multilingual LLM Evaluation

EMNLP 2026 (Main)

A large-scale region-aware factual benchmark covering 91 languages from 86 countries (127 language–country pairs) with over 220K curated samples drawn from native regional sources, disentangling linguistic proficiency from locally embedded cultural knowledge.

OpenReview Hugging Face Dataset

MultiCrowspairs & SHADES: Multilingual Stereotype Benchmarking

LREC-COLING 2024 · NAACL 2025

Multilingual and cross-cultural evaluation frameworks addressing the practical challenges of evaluating social stereotypes across global language communities, demonstrating how single-culture US-centric proxies fail to transfer across diverse socio-cultural settings.

LREC-COLING Paper NAACL SHADES Paper

CVQA: Culturally-Diverse Multilingual Visual Question Answering

NeurIPS 2024

A global visual question answering benchmark covering 30+ languages across multiple continents, curated by native speakers to evaluate how vision-language models generalize to culturally grounded images and knowledge worldwide.

Benchmark Website arXiv:2406.05967

CaMMT: Culturally Aware Multimodal Machine Translation

EMNLP Findings 2025

An evaluation benchmark measuring how effectively multimodal machine translation models understand and preserve culturally specific nuances, visual grounding, and idiomatic expressions across languages.

arXiv:2505.24456

La Leaderboard: Spanish Varieties & Regional Languages Benchmark

ACL 2025

A comprehensive open evaluation suite and leaderboard assessing LLM capabilities across dialectal varieties of Spanish and co-official regional languages of Spain and Latin America.

ACL Paper
