**La Leaderboard** is an open collaborative initiative to benchmark Large Language Models on Spanish varieties and regional languages across Spain and Latin America.

* **Publication**: ACL 2025 (*"La leaderboard: A large language model leaderboard for Spanish varieties and languages of Spain and Latin America"*).
* **Objective**: Evaluate how commercial and open-source models understand and generate different regional varieties of Spanish and related languages.
