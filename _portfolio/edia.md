**EDIA** (*Evaluación de Discriminación en Inteligencia Artificial*) is an open-source auditing platform developed by the Artificial Intelligence team at **Fundación Vía Libre** (where Guido Ivetta serves as Technical Lead).

* **EDIA 1.0 & EDIA 2.0 (Multimodal)**: Enables civil society organizations, educators, and discrimination experts to systematically uncover and measure demographic biases in AI systems without requiring advanced machine learning engineering skills.
* **Demonstrations & Workshops**: Featured as an interactive tutorial at **ACM FAccT 2024** in Rio de Janeiro (*"EDIA: Evaluation of Bias for Experts in Discrimination, not in Computer Science"*), presented at **RightsCon 2023** in Costa Rica, and utilized in nationwide teacher-training programs.
* **Platform**: [ia.vialibre.org.ar/edia1-0](https://ia.vialibre.org.ar/edia1-0/) and [ia.vialibre.org.ar/ediamultimodal2-0](https://ia.vialibre.org.ar/ediamultimodal2-0/)
