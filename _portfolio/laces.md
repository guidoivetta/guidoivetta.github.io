**LACES** addresses the critical geo-cultural gap in Natural Language Processing bias evaluations, where Latin American varieties and communities are historically under-represented.

* **Publication**: Findings of ACL 2026 (*"Adaptive Data Collection for Latin-American Community-sourced Evaluation of Stereotypes"*).
* **Methodology**: Implements an adaptive data collection protocol that iteratively expands and validates demographic stereotypes through community input.
