**INCLUDE-2.0** is an extensive multilingual benchmark for region-aware factual evaluation across global languages and cultures.

* **Publication**: EMNLP 2026 Main Conference (*"INCLUDE-2.0: Disentangling Knowledge Gaps in Multilingual LLM Evaluation"*).
* **Scale**: Covers **91 languages from 86 countries (127 total language–country pairs)** with over **220K curated samples** drawn from native regional sources.
* **Controlled Evaluation**: Annotates regional knowledge types and pairs items with English translations, allowing researchers to decouple language fluency from knowledge of locally embedded facts.
* **Resources**: [Hugging Face Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/epfl-nlp/include-2.0) | [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/forum?id=FbLlZwgUss)
