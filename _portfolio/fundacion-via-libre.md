Research collaboration with the **Ethics in AI** team at **Fundación Vía Libre**, a non-profit civil society organization in Argentina dedicated to digital rights and technological democracy.

* Developing empirical methodologies for characterizing social biases in NLP.
* Formulating guidelines for responsible deployment of AI in education, health, and public services.
* Engaging in policy discussions and participatory community workshops.
