**HESEIA** is a community-sourced dataset for auditing and evaluating social biases in Large Language Models. Unlike conventional top-down bias benchmarks, HESEIA was created through participatory co-design workshops directly within secondary school environments in Argentina.

* **Paper**: Published at EMNLP 2025 (*"HESEIA: A community-based dataset for evaluating social biases in large language models, co-designed in real school settings in Latin America"*).
* **Focus**: Real-world stereotypes, regional socio-cultural biases, and localized language varieties.
* **Impact**: Bridges the gap between affected communities and NLP evaluation methodologies.
