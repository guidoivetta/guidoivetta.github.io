**HESEIA** is a community-sourced dataset for auditing and evaluating social biases in Large Language Models. Unlike conventional top-down benchmarks, HESEIA was created through participatory co-design workshops directly within secondary school environments in Argentina and Latin America.

* **Scale & Participation**: Co-designed with **370 secondary school educators and over 5,000 students across 189 schools**, yielding **46,499 sentences** capturing intersectional and regionally grounded biases across curriculum subjects.
* **Publications & Presentations**:
  * Published and presented as an Oral presentation at **EMNLP 2025** in Suzhou, China (*"HESEIA: A community-based dataset for evaluating social biases in large language models, co-designed in real school settings in Latin America"*).
  * Selected for the AI Research & Impact Symposium at the **India AI Impact Summit 2026** in New Delhi, India.
* **Impact**: Bridges the gap between affected educational communities and NLP evaluation methodologies, uncovering biases that standard auto-debiasing prompts fail to mitigate.
