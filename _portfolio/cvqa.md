**CVQA** is a large-scale multimodal evaluation benchmark created to evaluate the cultural diversity and multilingual capabilities of Vision-Language Models (VLMs).

* **Publication**: NeurIPS 2024 (*"CVQA: Culturally-diverse Multilingual Visual Question Answering Benchmark"*).
* **Coverage**: Over 30 languages spanning multiple continents, featuring imagery and question-answer pairs curated by local native speakers and researchers worldwide.
* **Key Finding**: Highlights significant performance degradation in state-of-the-art multimodal models when queried on non-Western cultural concepts.
